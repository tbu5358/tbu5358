# Kanzlo

**LangChain-Based German Legal AI Assistant**  
Founder / Product Builder

Kanzlo is a German legal AI assistant built around LangChain-based assistant workflows.

The product is designed to help lawyers work with matters, documents, facts, risks, legal issues, drafts, and research in a source-grounded way. The core focus is trust: making AI outputs understandable, verifiable, and useful inside professional legal workflows.

---

## What It Does

Kanzlo helps lawyers:

- Understand matter files
- Extract and structure case facts
- Analyse uploaded legal documents
- Draft legal work product
- Review risks and missing evidence
- Maintain matter memory over time
- See where AI-generated information came from

---

## Key Work

- Built legal assistant architecture around LangChain-style orchestration.
- Designed matter memory for facts, parties, documents, legal issues, claims, risks, and next steps.
- Created UX concepts that show what the AI is working on, where information came from, and what the lawyer should verify.
- Designed assistant modes for drafting, reviewing, researching, checking, and strategy.
- Developed concepts for citation-aware legal workflows, source-backed outputs, and case-memory graphs.
- Worked through legal AI concerns around data retention, database access, training data, user trust, and regulated-domain reliability.
- Created detailed product specs, UX flows, implementation prompts, and system architecture documents.
